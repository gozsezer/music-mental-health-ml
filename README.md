# Music listening and self-reported depression/anxiety

**Status: documentation-only research portfolio entry; NOT an executable reproduction.**

## Study overview
Public Music and Mental Health Survey, n=736. Nested cross-validation; music-plus-age Random Forest mean ROC-AUC 0.621 (depression) and 0.601 (anxiety). Symptom-augmented models performed better but do not isolate music-specific information.
